October 2017: Farewell to Paradise

Chong Wang Lv6
  2017-10-22 22:30:09 2017-10-22 22:30:09 Created   2026-10-09 14:41:52 2026-10-09 14:41:52 Updated

No kidding. . . I plan to write longer this time. . . The previous monthly summary was very perfunctory. . .

My writing skills are almost 0. Some emotions, experiences, and thoughts are difficult to express elegantly in language after they are formed at the brain level. I am in a hurry. . . While my desire to talk is so strong tonight, I will quickly record it.

Ling Yang

I was just thinking about what to name October, and suddenly it occurred to me that I have been watching Dean recently, re-reading her “Farewell to Paradise”, “Western Jue”, “Dong Ni”, and finally finished “The Order of the South”. All the girls in her novels are full of energy. You may say they are willful or authoritative. Anyway, the kind of paranoia in the bones runs through the life of the characters and throughout the whole book. Di’an has great ambitions, which can be clearly felt from the beginning of Xijue and Nanfang Youlingyang, but she always doesn’t handle the ending very well, such as Nanyin, for example, Nanfang Youlingyang. After Nanfang Youlingyang, you can feel the author’s initial bravery and the difficulty of not being able to complete her original ambition in the end (I can especially understand…). Of course, her ability to describe characters is as good as ever. Five-star recommendation.

Useless

After coming back, I solved a lot of questions on 51nod. It was really fun. I had been holding back for a long time. . . Rank rose to 23 again. As expected, I really like answering questions. I have spent all my spare time answering questions in the past two years. It is really fun. . . But my IQ is too low. I have only heard that rng_58 makes a living by doing test questions for Atcoder. Dreamoon also mentioned this on Facebook. . . Who else can support themselves by doing questions every day? . . Don’t know anymore. . .

I regretted meeting the algorithm question too late, cursed that life was too short, and cursed that my IQ was too low. I started studying questions in 2015. At that time, I was preparing for my senior thesis. When I went to the Internet Research Institute for an interview, I didn’t know what a binary tree was, so I was kicked out. . . It would be great if I could be exposed to such fun things in high school. I guess I would have time to write some fun tools and the like.

Previously, an interviewer from a domestic company kept asking me what the use of doing these questions was, as they were not needed for work.

Because it’s cool.

Just like you like watching movies and playing games, I have been looking for more than 20 years to find such an obsessed hobby. Please do me a favor and stop asking back and forth whether it is useful or not. What is the use of watching movies?

Of course, I have to admit that my IQ is very low. . . Cry. . .

Ashes

The eleven who came back in October spent 10 days in the dormitory studying questions. . . It was purely for the interview round at Google on October 10th (the first round of positive interviews was on September 18th, and the second round was on October 10th, Hmm…). I was very nervous during the interview. Later I realized that the question was actually a pretty good question, but when I was optimizing, my thinking got stuck. Fortunately, I figured it out before the interviewer prompted me. Regardless of the outcome, the internship at Google in 2017 has finally come to an end, and it is engraved in my memory and shining brightly.

To sum up this period of work, I really tried my best. . . But the final results of the internship were not very satisfactory to me. . .

Now, I have been defeated. I used the moth I used to fly to the flame in exchange for the handful of warm ashes I hold in my hand today. Fortunately, I have not forgotten that the name of this ashes is ideal.

From “Farewell to Paradise”

At first I wanted to be like senior Eurce.

If I still fail to achieve my goal after fighting hard to the end again, I will not be able to accept this failure and completely throw myself out.

Chatting with a senior at Google, “Google should not be the target, Google is a path. What kind of talent you want to be in the future is the target. There is no need to take the result of getting into Google too seriously.”

I thought to myself: I don’t think it’s important whether I can become a regular employee. . . only. . . This affirmation. . For me, who has suffered from low self-esteem in recent years, it is very important. . .

Recently

51nod can only do this, because in October I went back to Toutiao for internship. . . (Maybe he will go back to school soon…?)

I don’t know if I can graduate. . .

I have learned a lot, but I am also doubting myself and wondering whether I can become a qualified programmer. . .

Don’t know what the future will look like.

In the past two years, my mentality has changed a lot. I have met many powerful people. I boast that my mental endurance has become very strong, and I boast that my steel heart continues to grow. However. . . Low self-esteem is also growing, as is anxiety, and you will be at a loss. In the past, you would complain to anyone you caught, but now you are looking for more friends and parents. But today I was thinking, some negative emotions should not be complained to friends all day long. They cause a lot of trouble to others. This needs to be digested by oneself, and the pressure also needs to be resolved by oneself. In the future, we must enhance our ability in this area. After all, if this is the path you choose to take, you should smash everything into pieces and swallow it in your stomach, and give back to the world around you “I’m okay, don’t worry about me, let’s continue.”

To be frank, I don’t really love this so-called wonderful world and wonderful life. When I grit my teeth and continue walking, there are always two kinds of villains who keep bb. . One said, “I understand everything. Keep working hard.” The other said, “No matter what, it’s still a living thing.” . .

Anyway, I wish you good luck today and hopefully this week.

Some day I wish I could be someone like you.

I don’t know if I expressed my emotions gracefully this time.
