2017_December_My failure and continuation

Chong Wang Lv6
  2017-12-25 21:52:15 2017-12-25 21:52:15 Created   2026-10-09 08:47:27 2026-10-09 08:47:27 Updated

Strong feelings, anxiety, wanting to take off, explode on the spot, stay alone for a while.

He is still a positive person, full of hope every morning, and has his own battlefield every day.

//It’s far from finished. I just used it as a test today. . .

//Update：2018-1-16 23:06

Like 2011 and 2015, 2017 is destined to be written in 2997ms of life. Everything in this year can be described as wonderful, including so-called failures.

The idea for the title comes from Rene Liu’s “My Failure and Greatness”. When I first saw this title, I thought it was great. Then I read the activity sheets and diaries for the 12 months of this year. Replace “great” with “continue” and find that it fits this year’s situation perfectly.

It seems that I just had to sort out some things about turning twenty-four in October. Thinking about that time, my mentality exploded. . . I wonder how I am such a bad person all day long, which can be said to be a constant state of panic. Now that I have passed the age of twenty-four, it is time to write “Toward Thirty-five”. Think about it, thirty-five is really not that far away. I’m writing something, but how can I go off topic so badly in just a few sentences? . .

Studying for graduate school in this school was a very helpless decision. At that time, I didn’t know anything as an undergraduate, and I had no ideas at all. Following the popular trend, I got a postgraduate degree and enrolled in a graduate school. When I was 18 years old, I did not do well in the college entrance examination. At that time, my parents gave me a way out and said that I would repeat my studies. Thinking about the hard work and fatigue that would drive me crazy during my senior year of high school, I decided to leave and never repeat my studies. Now I think about how correct it is. I don’t have much time to spend such a good year on the tedium and hardships of senior year. This little time refers to this life.

When I was young, I always felt that I had a lot of time but very little money. I took the green leather train to meet my classmates, and sat on the hard seat until I doubted my life. I had a standing ticket to go home on November 1, and couldn’t sleep at all in the middle of the night. Nowadays, time is scarce and money is still scarce.

After taking a look at what I wrote this year, I feel a lot of self-pity and self-pity, and I constantly complain about my own mistakes. Do you want to improve? have. Then in the process of improvementmaybe I met some outstanding people, so I became even more convinced that I was good at it.

Writing also requires talent. After so many years of writing, I still don’t have a main line at all. Every time I finish writing, I feel like it’s not expressed clearly, but I don’t know where to change it. Overall it is disorganized.

I particularly care about other people’s opinions, so I occasionally get a sense of vanity. Most of the hard experiences are my own choices. I live a very tiring life. I hope next year will be simpler.

Now I am sitting in the library writing my graduation thesis for the graduate school. The books in front of me are books on data structures, computer networks, operating systems and other professional courses for the postgraduate entrance examination. Thinking about it, I shouldn’t stay in the graduate school. . . It seems that I don’t have any of this knowledge. . .

You were so young, but you didn’t know it.

After I came back in October, I immediately went to Toutiao for an internship. For the next ten days or so, I watched “Hello, Old Times” all day long. . . This drama is good, even though it has a lot of Mary Sues and stuff. . .

Tokyo is not far, thirty is not far.

I can’t let go now, and I will never let go in the future.

I wish you good health, a smooth graduation, and all the best in 2018.

And time is leaving at a racing speed

I originally wanted to summarize it well, but it is now 22:51 on January 16th. I have never used the lunar calendar as a scale to mark the beginning and end of a year. One year is just one year. In other words, 16/365 has passed in 2018.

At night, I opened a game and played it for a while, then I lay in bed and couldn’t sleep again. Just thinking that if you don’t like a job, you can change it. If you don’t like playing a game, don’t play it. If I don’t play this game for the time being, I’ll save it. But what about this life? If you don’t like it anymore and don’t make any plans, what’s going on? One day we will have to leave this life, and one day our whole body will be filled with tubes. Some people even have no chance to have all the tubes inserted and leave in a hurry. So how can life be meaningful?

So at this time, I really had the feeling of “sorrowing for the brief moment of my life, and envying the infinity of the Yangtze River.” But sometimes it is precisely because this life is so limited that we have so many feelings? Is that really the case?

Su Zi said: “You also know that water and the moon are like this? Those who have passed away are like this, but they have not yet gone; those who have waxed and waned are like that, but their deaths have not waxed and waned. If you look at the general from its change, then the heaven and earth cannot last for an instant; look at it from its unchangeableness, then things and I are endless, so why envy you! And! Between heaven and earth, everything has its own owner. Even if it is not my own, I can’t take any of it. But the breeze on the river and the bright moon on the mountain can be heard by the ears and become colors by the eyes. They can be taken without restraint and used inexhaustibly. This is the endless treasure of the Creator, and I and my son are in harmony.”

I want to know whether the old man Su Dongpo really thinks so.

Then at this time, sleep finally came. The couple and their pillows were spread out in the boat, unaware of the whiteness of the east.

In fact, it’s not bad, I will live well every day. How should I put it, in my personal time coordinate, 2011, 2015, and 2017 are all very important years. I left that small town in 2011. In 2015 I found my daily battleground. In 2017, I knew that I finally knew who I wanted to be and what I wanted to fill my life with. Even though it came very late, many years later than others, I am still fascinated by its arrival.

In fact, it’s not that I won’t update this blog post, it’s just that I have been busy with my graduate thesis recently, and that’s what I have been doing until now.

It seems that every farewell is a bit rushed and it is difficult to say a perfect farewell. That’s it, 2017.
