At this point, I can’t help but applaud my strong learning ability, it’s so awesome. After that, it is obvious to find the IP and port, just in bind or connect, the result is. . . Things that made me miserable came one after another.

The information given by msdn is extremely limited. According to the log of the program call function, it can be known that NtDeviceIoControlFile is called multiple times in an execution interval of the program, and there is not much correlation between the previous behavior and the subsequent behavior, so the IP and port information may only exist in NtDeviceIoControlFile.

Then judging the behavior, we know that the information should exist in IO_AFD_BIND, IO_AFD_SET_CONNECT, IO_AFD_CONNECT.

I didn’t know anything about AFD before, until I found this blog, and learned that AFD is a structure that exists between the socket interface and the TDI interface command. It is essentially a TDI client that implements the TCP/IP function of the network component by calling the TDI interface at the bottom. Of course, this article is about controlling traffic through Hooks, and has nothing to do with what I want to do.

OK, now I find that BIND and CONNECT do not have suitable structs. . . A search on Google yielded very little information, and I found [this] article on the forum (http://www.debugman.com/archiver/tid/3715). Wow, this is the struct of AFD_BIND? Then I found TRANSPORT_ADDRESS on msdn, and finally TA_ADDRESS here. I output the Address according to the instructions below, and found that the result is garbled. . .

At this time, I found a [post] (http://www.lai18.com/content/8234443.html) about TDI filtering to obtain IP and port on the forum, and learned about the method of obtaining IP and port.

I personally don’t understand one thing about this code. If the port in the data is a char type, the final addition must be within 254. The final port result output is indeed a very small number. The IP and port are obtained in this way, but the result is wrong.

I searched again and found this [post] (http://bbs.pediy.com/archive/index.php?t-150693.html), which talks about using ntohs to convert network byte order to host byte order. I was very excited when I saw it yesterday and thought it would definitely work, but the output was still wrong = =.

In addition, later on, I also found an [afd manual] (https://doxygen.reactos.org/d7/dd6/struct__AFD__MAPBUF.html), which was like a treasure that contained many definitions of AFD. Based on this, I am more convinced that the method I used should be correct, and I did get the result, but the result is not correct no matter how I test = =.

As I write this, I realize that I have written very little. The first month has passed like this. The exam is about to begin, and I don’t know if Cairu will graduate. . I have to learn DECAF and QEMU tomorrow. I have been clamoring to watch it but haven’t watched much. Every time I make a plan, I am full of hope and confidence, as if I will definitely be able to cross the finish line with a lot of luggage on my back. However, every time I reach the finish line, I often pick up the wreckage of disappointment. However, despite this, I am still very optimistic and still full of fighting spirit on the next track. I read “Happy Linux” this month and found this book to be really good. . . Playing codingame. Most of September was spent feeding the dogs again, no matter what, good or bad, it was a delicacy. October continues to hurt.
