2020-September-Back to the original place

Chong Wang Lv6
  2020-09-24 15:18:09 2020-09-24 15:18:09 Created   2026-10-09 08:47:47 2026-10-09 08:47:47 Updated

Returning to the beginning is the title of Chapter 1 of the novel “Western Finals”. Dean’s early and middle books are not to mention very good-looking, but they are very top-notch and have great staying power.

I seem to have been lamenting the dissatisfaction in life. In the past two days, I watched the Chinese men’s football team and I was rolling on the bed with laughter. Later, the official account was changed to Just Lose It, and I felt that I really got the essence.

The last time I saw the Chinese team play so well was the last time.

What a fucking philosopher.

We suddenly discovered that regret, unwillingness, and disappointment from the heart turned out to be precious emotions, because they represent that we no longer have desires and desires. It’s like when you see hope, life gives you a sap. At least it means that you can still see hope, and at least life is still willing to take advantage of you.

Even though my football career at Peking University was unremarkable and awkward and boring, I still love football. I enjoy every time I run on the court and I think this is enough. Many people from Peking University laugh at themselves, saying that getting admitted to Peking University may be the pinnacle of our lives. Today many of us are about to graduate, and some of them are about to enter society. In fact, I have figured it out now. For most people, there are far more failures than successes, but so what, failure is not shameful. Peking University will always be great, but I will still be ordinary. Finally, I would like to share with you a piece of text from our official account, dedicated to every graduating classmate. The meaning of the Chinese men’s football team is basically this. It teaches us failure, teaches us mediocrity, and teaches us to face reality, but no matter what, who says success at the age of 18 is not success. Now that I have stood at the top, why should I be afraid of the endless abyss? There is joy in retreating even an inch.

There is joy in retreating an inch. It would be nice if I wrote my official account as seriously as others, but my life is still too rough. . . .

I watched two movies in total this year, one was “囧Mom”, which I watched in Xigua during the Spring Festival, and the other was “Jiang Ziya” which I watched while my elder sister was getting married. Both of them almost made me fall asleep.

I didn’t watch the movie “Win the Championship”, but there was a line in the ending song “River of Life” that struck me again.

I used to be hesitant and confused, not wanting to turn into powder. I used to be inexplicably persistent, regardless of the consequences.

I am reading “The Courage to Be Disliked” and “Nonlinear Growth”. Both books are good, especially the first one. I really like discussing some serious issues in a conversational way. If you say that Li Dan’s “The Laughter Field” did not draw on this idea, I don’t believe it. Heavy things should be handled with care.

I have finished reading “Klein Pot”, a magical book. “Inception” appeared thirty years ago. This novel is much deeper than “Inception”. Because “Inception” is a movie, there are many places that cannot be explored in depth. It is a wonderful movie made through the shell of this stage. This novel is different. It directly discusses which part of this stage is reality and which part is not. It is also very exciting.

Work has been quite annoying recently. I just can’t figure out a small problem, there’s no progress, I still have to report it, and I have to go to meetings pretending to be a mature middle-aged person with normal emotions. In fact, my heart is almost shattered.

Finally, let’s talk about something happy. TC has finally opened up. The flag that was set up at the beginning of the year has finally been removed. I am still very happy looking at this profile. Although it is only 50 dollars now, this hope is a beginning and will bear fruit next year.

I have finally learned a lot about the new platform, and I will continue to maintain it and continue to be anxious. The long week is over, and a new week is yet another long one.

Question: What do you think is the ideal happiness?

Answer: Take control of your own life and be a free waste
