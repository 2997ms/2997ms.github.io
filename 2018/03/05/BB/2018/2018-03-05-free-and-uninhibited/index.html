March 2018: Free and Uninhibited

Chong Wang Lv6
  2018-03-05 14:58:02 2018-03-05 14:58:02 Created   2026-10-09 14:41:52 2026-10-09 14:41:52 Updated

The theme of March 2018 is farewell. I once dreamed of leaving this school. When I was in my first year of graduate school, I kept thinking about dropping out, like yanyiwu did. But because I really didn’t have the courage, I spent all my time hesitating and waiting. At the end of March, when everything ended and I had to leave, I realized that I had left so much love here.

The same goes for this blog post. I wrote some of it very early on, but because of work and because I found that I had nothing to say, I didn’t continue.

Today I hope to finish it and write down the whole feeling.

I can’t wait any longer

This should be the last time I sit on the third floor of Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications’ library. I couldn’t finish “The Shadow of the Wind” and “The Book of Lost Things” until the end, and I still haven’t finished them eight years later.

What does this feel like? It’s not as heartbreaking as when I graduated from high school. Leaving this time makes me feel like the passage of time, the water is flowing over my body bit by bit, and the years have stolen away 7 years of time. Now my hometown is still far away, but as a young person, you have already passed away. Unlike any other period of time, this is a time that I especially want to keep returning to. It is a video that keeps playing in my memory. I now envy people who have middle school years, but I don’t want to go back at all. This recent period is just the opposite. I want to keep going back and see if I can do better in that gap.

I was wandering around the campus today and saw that people born in 2000 have already entered this school. People are often forced to leave a place. When I left, I obviously felt that I was being urged by these kids - you should leave quickly. Later, I thought, when we leave this world, looking at the world smaller than ourselves, it seems that we can only say goodbye silently, and then go on to the next journey.

Because I have to change my dressing tomorrow, I went back to campus tonight. Riding a bicycle on campus feels like a world away. The campus card has not been demagnetized yet, and there are still people living in the dormitory of the third year student.

After eating, I went to Xu31 and found that the rooms in Xu31 were all for students studying for a master’s and doctoral degree, and 307 of Xu3 was completely empty. Nothing that belonged to me in Beijing Postal Service is gone. I had to go back to 608 again. Fortunately, because it was Sunday, there was no one there tonight. You can enjoy the Internet and this sea-like silence by yourself. There is a lot of traffic and traffic outside the window.

April has just arrived, and the weather has changed completely. You can wear short sleeves. It seems that summer has just arrived.

Two days later the temperature dropped to 0 degrees. . .

Before going to work, I went to school to change the dressing. It was supposed to be a dressing change, but the doctor said I should forget about removing the stitches. I thought, OK, OK. It just so happens that I never have to come again.

I didn’t expect to meet Y at Beijing Postal University. I simply replied on WeChat and found out that he was still in school. I lamented the seven years with him for a while, and then we had to say goodbye for twenty minutes. Hope to see you later.

Something that moved me. I was chatting with my mother in the evening and mentioned that I was moving out of the dormitory recently. My mother told me not to throw away the quilt.

Me: “Which quilt?”

Mom: “It’s the one your grandma and I specially beat with cotton before you left.”

I searched through my memory and suddenly remembered that there was such a thing. Unexpectedly, she still remembered the quilt.

That quilt was made after I took the college entrance examination in 2011. My mother and grandma were afraid that I would be cold here, so they asked someone to make it. It was very thick. At that time, my mother did not know that the temperature here in Beijing did not require this. She brought quilts, clothes (many of which were not worn at all in the end), and even green cotton slippers made by my grandmother. The luggage was heavy and heavy. The three of us each carried two large bags of stuff, which was like a human express delivery. It was very troublesome to get there. I remember that when we went to the hotel, our stuff was all over the floor, and there was no place for us to stay.

There is no need for so many quilts here. Beijing is very hot and very dry.

Thinking of the quilt that I threw away because it was too heavy when I moved to the headquarters in my sophomore year, I couldn’t help it when we were chatting. My mother still remembers such a thing after 8 years.

Free and carefree

My life ideal when I was 17 years old: “Be free and live a wild life.” This is still the case for now, but we are far from this ideal.

The specific picture formed by these eight words in my mind is this: it is snowing heavily outside, and it is very, very warm inside the house, always warm. There is so much food, there is always enough. I just lay on the sofa, covered with a thin quilt, eating snacks and watching movies. It’s snowing heavily outside for one day, two days, or next year. I don’t care. I only care about snacks and fire. As for whether I have enough money, I don’t care. I only care about snacks and fire. When I reach a certain age, I don’t care whether I get married and have children. I’ve said that I’m going to be wild. I don’t care. I only care about snacks and fire. I just stayed in my house for a day or two until I died.

The ideal I want to achieve is to be able to do what I like in the house without being restricted by others. Others’ eyes cannot hurt me. I can be free and unrestrained. Watching the sun rise and set, and the snowflakes falling, I still only care about my snacks and the fire.
